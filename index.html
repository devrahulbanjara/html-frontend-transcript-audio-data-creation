Speak naturally — pretend you're talking to a real person

Read at your normal conversational pace. Don't slow down, over-enunciate, or sound robotic. Natural rhythm and intonation is exactly what the model needs to learn from.

Read the sentence silently first, then hit Record. Stop only after you've finished the last word. Find a quiet spot and keep your device at a consistent distance.
